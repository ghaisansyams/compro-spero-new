Vocabulary Trainer with IELTS Writing Marking

A vocabulary trainer built around a 148-entry PDF that stays the source of truth: every row is transcribed verbatim — pronunciation, part of speech, three meanings, synonyms and examples with Indonesian translations — while AI enrichment lives in a separate layer and incomplete rows are flagged "Needs Review" instead of invented. Manajemen Pesantren

Whole-School ERP for an Islamic Vocational Boarding School

The system a whole school runs on, built across fourteen sprints. One student record feeds every module: enrolment and family relations, timetables and grades, RFID attendance, dormitory beds, Qur'an memorisation tracked to the individual verse, a double-entry accounting engine, laundry, procurement and asset depreciation, a read-only parent portal, a 42-KPI executive dashboard, and an event-driven notification engine. Student Prepaid-Wallet ERP + POS Integration

Sari Roti

Corporate Landing Page Rebuilt from a Figma Prototype

A corporate landing page rebuilt from a Figma prototype by reading the file through the Figma REST API instead of eyeballing screenshots — so every value in the code is sourced rather than guessed. The type scale and six brand colours come straight from the node tree. Product photos are cropped by each fill's own imageTransform matrix, not object-cover, which is the difference between the product filling the frame and the shot showing an empty kitchen counter. The three marquees run at 10s linear because that is the Smart Animate duration the prototype actually declares — including the two vertical career columns, motion that is invisible in a static export and only shows up in the interaction data. The curved product strip is a ring mask struck from two radii of a giant circle. Desktop and mobile both match their own Figma frame, and 74 MB of raw assets ship as 1.4 MB of WebP.

01Game

Tower Defense

A grid tower defense built entirely from primitives — boxes, cones and cylinders, told apart by colour, silhouette and emissive glow rather than by any imported model. Six towers, each with three upgrade tiers and four targeting modes, hold a serpentine trench carved across a 16×16 board against eight enemy types that each demand a different answer: armour that blunts small hits and rewards heavy ones, splitters that break in two on death, healers that quietly undo your damage, and a 1,500-HP Colossus immune to every slow in the game. Two abilities on cooldown — a 120-damage airstrike and a board-wide deep freeze — are the counterplay when the boss walks. One central loop drives every entity, so fifty of them still hold 60fps.

Aether
