Manajemen Pesantren

Whole-School ERP for an Islamic Vocational Boarding School

The system a whole school runs on, built across fourteen sprints. One student record feeds every module: enrolment and family relations, timetables and grades, RFID attendance, dormitory beds, Qur'an memorisation tracked to the individual verse, a double-entry accounting engine, laundry, procurement and asset depreciation, a read-only parent portal, a 42-KPI executive dashboard, and an event-driven notification engine. Skill Village Wallet

Student Prepaid-Wallet ERP + POS Integration

A ledger-first prepaid-wallet ERP for an Islamic boarding school. Finance tops up student balances offline, the Skillage Mart POS charges wallets at checkout through an idempotent API (charge-before-commit with automatic reversal on failure), and parents track every rupiah in a read-only portal. Money lives in an append-only Postgres ledger moved only by atomic row-locked RPCs, with multi-tenant RLS end-to-end — plus finance dashboards, reconciliation, reports, an audit trail and an event-driven notification centre.

Sari Roti

Corporate Landing Page Rebuilt from a Figma Prototype

A corporate landing page rebuilt from a Figma prototype by reading the file through the Figma REST API instead of eyeballing screenshots — so every value in the code is sourced rather than guessed. The type scale and six brand colours come straight from the node tree. Product photos are cropped by each fill's own imageTransform matrix, not object-cover, which is the difference between the product filling the frame and the shot showing an empty kitchen counter. The three marquees run at 10s linear because that is the Smart Animate duration the prototype actually declares — including the two vertical career columns, motion that is invisible in a static export and only shows up in the interaction data. The curved product strip is a ring mask struck from two radii of a giant circle. Desktop and mobile both match their own Figma frame, and 74 MB of raw assets ship as 1.4 MB of WebP.

01 / Game

Tower Defense

A grid tower defense built entirely from primitives — boxes, cones and cylinders, told apart by colour, silhouette and emissive glow rather than by any imported model. Six towers, each with three upgrade tiers and four targeting modes, hold a serpentine trench carved across a 16×16 board against eight enemy types that each demand a different answer: armour that blunts small hits and rewards heavy ones, splitters that break in two on death, healers that quietly undo your damage, and a 1,500-HP Colossus immune to every slow in the game. Two abilities on cooldown — a 120-damage airstrike and a board-wide deep freeze — are the counterplay when the boss walks. One central loop drives every entity, so fifty of them still hold 60fps.

  • React Three Fiber
  • TypeScript
  • Zustand
  • Postprocessing

Diecast Rally

A toy car on a sun-baked playmat at golden hour: collect twelve coins as fast as you can, then race your own best time. The car is a real raycast vehicle with per-wheel suspension — it rolls on landings, washes wide when you carry too much speed into a corner, and drifts when the handbrake cuts rear grip. 10 / 3D & Creative

Aether

A hollow shell of 3000 glowing particles steered with bare hands — move to carry it, roll your wrist to spin it, spread both palms to swell it, hold up one to five fingers to shift its hue. Pinch and hold to charge a ring, then release: the shell throws itself across the screen and springs back together, a partial charge giving a proportionally weaker pulse. Every particle is its own damped spring on the CPU, and a One-Euro filter keeps the tracking still when your hand is still without lagging when it moves. One HTML file, no build step.

Aether

A hollow shell of 3000 glowing particles steered with bare hands — move to carry it, roll your wrist to spin it, spread both palms to swell it, hold up one to five fingers to shift its hue. Pinch and hold to charge a ring, then release: the shell throws itself across the screen and springs back together, a partial charge giving a proportionally weaker pulse. Every particle is its own damped spring on the CPU, and a One-Euro filter keeps the tracking still when your hand is still without lagging when it moves. One HTML file, no build step.

  • Three.js
  • MediaPipe Hands
  • GLSL
  • One-Euro Filter
